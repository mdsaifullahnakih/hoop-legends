## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- Two-player, turn-based 3D basketball game built with Three.js
- Scoring: +10 per basket, −10 per miss (never below 0)
- Win by reaching 100 points, or by leading when the 2:30 clock ends; equal scores are a draw
- Drag-to-aim controls for touch and mouse, keyboard controls and a vertical power meter
- Live scoreboard, match clock, shot feedback, victory fireworks and applause
- Sound effects with a global Sound on/off toggle
- Welcome-screen background music (embedded mp3) that loops on the home screen and stops as soon as the match starts
- Gaming-style **PLAY GAME** button with notched corners, glow and shine animation
- Responsive layout for phones, tablets and desktops in portrait and landscape
- Project documentation: README, LICENSE, CONTRIBUTING and CHANGELOG
